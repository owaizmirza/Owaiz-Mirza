# IT Demand Intake — Business Analysis Case Study

**Independent portfolio simulation.** No real stakeholders, enterprise system deployment, or measured business outcomes are claimed.

## Business problem
A fictional IT team handles technology requests through email, chat, and spreadsheets. Requests arrive incomplete, ownership is uncertain, and stakeholders cannot reliably track their status.

## Objective
Design an intake process that validates information, assigns an owner, supports prioritization, and maintains a status trail.

## Illustrative targets (not achieved outcomes)
At least 95% of submissions pass required-field validation; every accepted request has an accountable owner; 90% of complete requests are triaged within two business days.
