# 05 — UAT Plan

**Purpose:** Verify that the proposed intake workflow supports the fictional business scenarios and agreed requirements.

## Entry criteria
Requirements reviewed; stable test environment; test roles available; test data prepared; critical blocking defects resolved.

## Test approach
Execute positive and negative cases for form validation, unique IDs, owner assignment, status transitions, access control, and reporting.

## Defect fields
Test ID, severity, description, steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual, owner, status, evidence, retest result.

## Exit criteria (proposed)
All Must-have cases pass; no open Critical or High business-blocking defects; business owner approves residual known issues.

See [UAT cases](uat-test-cases.csv) and [traceability](requirements-traceability.csv). These are **designed test cases, not evidence of executed testing**.
