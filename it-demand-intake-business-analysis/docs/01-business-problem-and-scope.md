# 01 — Business Problem and Scope

## Problem statement
A fictional enterprise IT team receives requests through multiple unstructured channels. The lack of consistent required information and ownership contributes to repeated follow-ups, duplicate work, and limited visibility.

## Goals
1. Establish a single, structured submission channel.
2. Capture enough information for initial assessment.
3. Assign one accountable triage owner.
4. Make request status understandable to business requesters.
5. Report intake volume, quality, and triage timeliness.

## In scope
Form fields and validation; intake queue; categories and priority; ownership assignment; status workflow; duplicate warnings; audit history; operational reporting; UAT.

## Out of scope
Procurement, staffing approvals, implementation of a real ServiceNow instance, delivery of requested solutions, and enterprise identity integration.

## Stakeholders (fictional roles)
Business requester, business sponsor, IT intake analyst, service owner, solution architect, delivery lead, reporting analyst.

## Assumptions and risks
Priorities and ownership routing require agreement before implementation. Reporting metrics require a consistent reporting calendar, event timestamps, and definitions. Not every similar-looking demand is a duplicate.
